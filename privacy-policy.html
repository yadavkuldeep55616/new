2. Information we collect

Please do not send us account passwords, full card numbers, one-time security codes or government identification numbers — we do not need them and will delete them if received.

  • Technical and usage information. IP address, device and browser type, referring page or advertisement, the pages you view on this site, how long you stay, and a cookie identifier.

4. Cookies and similar technologies

A cookie is a small file stored by your browser. This site uses three kinds:

  • Essential cookies — needed for the site to work, including remembering your cookie notice choice. These cannot be switched off through the notice.
  • Analytics cookies — help us understand which pages are used and where visitors arrive from.
  • Advertising and measurement cookies — set by us and by Google to measure the performance of our Google Ads campaigns, including conversion measurement and, where enabled, remarketing.

You can block or delete cookies at any time in your browser settings; some parts of the site may then not work as intended. To opt out of advertising personalisation, use the links in section 5.

5. Google Ads, Google Tag Manager and Google Analytics

This website loads Google tags, including the Google Ads tag (AW-18451557443) and a Google Tag Manager container (GTM-KNG57GDP). We use them to measure the performance of our advertising — for example, to record when a visitor calls our support number after seeing our advertisement — and to understand how the site is used.

As a result, Google may set or read cookies on our behalf and may receive information about your visit, including your IP address and the pages you view, as an independent controller of that data for its own purposes. Google's own handling of data is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

You can control advertising personalisation and measurement here:

  • Google Ads Settings — review or switch off personalised advertising.
  • Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.
  • optout.aboutads.info — opt out of interest-based advertising from participating companies.
  • youronlinechoices.com — EU/UK choice tool.

6. Who we share information with

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers who process it on our terms and only as needed:

  • our hosting and email providers, to run this website and answer your messages;
  • our telephone and call-recording providers, to operate our support line;
  • our customer-relationship tools, to keep a record of your enquiry;
  • our payment processor, to take payment for anything you buy from us;
  • Google, for advertising and analytics as described in section 5;
  • professional advisers, insurers, or a buyer in the event of a business sale;
  • law enforcement, regulators or courts where we are legally required to disclose.

7. International transfers

We are based in India and some of our providers are located in other countries, including the United States. Where personal information is transferred internationally, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as the UK/EU Standard Contractual Clauses or an adequacy decision.

8. How long we keep information

  • Enquiries that do not lead to a purchase: 12 months, then deleted.
  • Customer and order records: 6 years after the last transaction, for tax and consumer-law purposes.
  • Call recordings: 12 months.
  • Consent records for calls, texts and email: 6 years after the consent was given or withdrawn.
  • Analytics and advertising data: retained in aggregate for up to 26 months.
  • Server and security logs: 90 days.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to: be informed; access a copy of your information; correct inaccurate information; delete it; restrict or object to our processing; receive it in a portable format; withdraw consent at any time; and lodge a complaint with your data-protection authority.

To exercise any right, email support@satardutechnology.com or write to us at the address in section 1. We respond within 30 days (45 days where the California rules apply) and we will tell you if we need more time.

10. Your privacy choices (Do Not Sell or Share)

We do not sell personal information for money. However, the use of advertising and analytics cookies described in sections 4 and 5 may count as a "sale" or "sharing" for targeted advertising under some US state privacy laws, including the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended. You can opt out by using the links in section 5, by clearing our cookies and reloading, or by emailing support@satardutechnology.com with the subject "Do Not Sell or Share". We honour opt-out preference signals, including the Global Privacy Control, where your browser sends one. You will not be discriminated against for exercising your rights.

10a. Security

This site is served over HTTPS. We use access controls, encryption in transit and provider-side encryption at rest, and we limit access to your information to the staff who need it. No system is perfectly secure. We do not ask for, and do not want, your passwords or one-time security codes.

11. Children

Our service is for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18, and we do not direct advertising to them.
