Complaints procedure

  1. Tell us. Call +1 (201) 279-1152 or email support@satardutechnology.com with "complaint" in the subject line. Please include any order or reference number you have.
  2. We acknowledge your complaint within 2 business days.
  3. We investigate and give you a substantive written response within 14 days, explaining what we found and what we will do.
  4. If you are still unhappy, tell us within 14 days and someone who was not involved in the original decision will review it and reply within a further 14 days.
  5. Refunds. If your complaint is about a charge from us, the rules in our Terms of Service apply.
